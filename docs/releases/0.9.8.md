# GMT 0.9.8 — Weave & Mandelbulb3D

Released [unreleased — pending push]. 183 commits since 0.9.7 (2026-06-25).
User-facing notes; the engineering log lives in git history and `docs/CHANGELOG_DEV.md`.

## Weave — combine formulas into new fractals

The headline of this release. The new **Weave** section in the Formula panel
stacks any formulas into a single fractal — built-in GMT formulas, community
Fragmentarium/DEC formulas, and imported Mandelbulb3D formulas, mixed freely.

- **Two timing modes.** *Sequence* runs formulas as ordered steps with loops
  and a "stop at" cut; *Rhythm* layers formulas on independent
  every-Nth-iteration patterns. Convert between the two without losing the
  pattern.
- **Every woven formula keeps its own sliders**, grouped per formula in the
  panel — all live (no recompile), all animatable, all modulation targets,
  with per-formula randomize.
- **Keyframes and LFOs follow their formulas.** Reordering or restructuring a
  weave offers to move existing animation with the slots it drives.
- **Hybrid Box presets** enter the weave as editable structure, and the
  classic interlace / Hybrid Box interleave scenes from older versions load
  and migrate automatically — same look, now editable as weaves.
- **Type-to-search** in the formula picker, and the picker's catalog gained a
  "weavable only" filter.

## Mandelbulb3D import

- **Load `.m3p` parameter files** from classic Mandelbulb3D. The importer
  reconstructs the fractal in GMT with live parameters — including hybrid
  scenes that weave several formulas.
- **A Mandelbulb3D catalog in the formula picker**: 180 converted formulas
  and 38 ready-to-load classic sample scenes, thumbnailed.
- **The ray marcher now steps the way Mandelbulb3D does**, so imports match
  the source look — and native scenes gained more consistent surface detail
  from the same change.

## Sky, fog & background overhaul

- **The backdrop is the sky.** Solid colors, gradients, images and HDR
  environments live in one Background & Sky section, and lighting reads from
  whatever you pick.
- **Sky Library**: 4 bundled HDR skies, one click to apply; skies you upload
  persist locally.
- **Fog follows the sky** by default — per-direction fog color sampled from
  the environment — with a dual-thumb fog range and a cleaner control layout.
- Smoother sky rendering: better filtering, the horizontal seam is gone, and
  camera blur on the backdrop actually reads as depth.

## Reflections

- **True multi-bounce reflections** with accurate colors, and fog now applies
  along reflected rays too.
- Less reflection noise (graded confidence fade instead of hard speckle), and
  the compile cost of turning reflections on dropped from 42s to 5s.

## Shadows at depth

- Shadow rays are now zoom-normalized: shadows no longer silently fade out on
  deep zooms. What you light is what you see, at any magnification.

## Share a scene by link

- **Free, no-sign-in sharing.** Any scene — including weaves and Mandelbulb3D
  imports — behind a short `?s=` link: one click to copy, opens straight into
  the editor. Signed-in shares are managed under "My Fractals".

## New formulas

- **Julia3DKucera** and **Julia3DZorich** — two constructions from the
  genuinely-3D quadratic Julia/Mandelbrot research program (Kučera's Bridges
  2019 circle map, and the Zorich–Mayer power maps). Their 3D
  Mandelbrot-class parameter bodies are, as far as we know, first-ever
  renders.

## Timeline & workflow

- Modifier-clicks and a right-click menu on keyframe diamonds; delete buttons
  on track groups and graph-editor rows; flat-track keyframes are selectable
  again.
- Custom viewport aspect ratio via a W×H popover at the top of the ratio
  menu; the adaptive-resolution icon now shows its state at a glance.
- Higher-contrast Preview shading, a Smooth Upscaling slider for Droste
  recursion, and studio-light add/drag/remove is undoable.

## Performance & fixes

- Recalling a saved camera renders as a smooth full-frame transition (no more
  banded strip during the move).
- Post-processing sliders (bloom, color) no longer rebuild the fractal image.
- Volumetric scattering compiles only when you enable it — no hidden cost
  from quality presets.
- Many UI consistency passes: one color picker, one slider system, unified
  panel chrome and steppers, and the UI theme is now composed from
  brightness / tint / contrast axes.
