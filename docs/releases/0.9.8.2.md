# GMT 0.9.8.2 — Repairs

Released September 1, 2026. 275 commits since 0.9.8.1 (2026-07-26).
User-facing notes; the engineering log is in [`docs/CHANGELOG_DEV.md`](../CHANGELOG_DEV.md)
and the ADRs.

Most of this release is invisible: a three-day audit of the whole codebase that
mainly produced documentation and tests. But it found real defects along the
way, and those are worth knowing about — particularly if you have old scenes,
have exported meshes, or have shared links.

## Things that were quietly broken

- **Opening the Camera Manager after loading an older scene no longer wipes your
  session.** Scenes saved before late April store their cameras in an older
  shape, and opening the panel with one loaded took the whole app down — blank
  screen, frozen render, unsaved work gone. Those cameras now load correctly.
- **Mesh exports were half a voxel off.** Every `.vdb` GMT has ever written was
  shifted by half a cell against the fractal it came from. Corrected — re-export
  anything you intend to line up precisely with other geometry.
- **Share links were dropping Droste settings and the Environment Profile.** Two
  features were competing for the same slot in the link format and one silently
  won. Existing links still open exactly as they did; new ones carry both.
- **Video export used the wrong frame rate whenever frame step was set.**
  Rendering every 2nd frame now produces a video that plays at half the rate, as
  it should, rather than one that runs fast.
- **Undo no longer loses audio clips**, and the FPS remap keeps your decks.
- **A failed export no longer hangs.** If the render worker crashed mid-export
  the job would sit there forever instead of reporting the failure.
- **Saved cameras with a blank name can be renamed again.** An empty label left
  a row with nothing to double-click, so it was stuck that way permanently.

## Rendering

- **Mirror-like reflections no longer show hard stair-stepped edges.** On
  polished curved surfaces, bright highlights in a reflection broke into hard
  2-pixel steps that never smoothed out however long you let a render
  accumulate. Also gone: a narrow band of flat grey down the seam of reflected
  environment maps.
- **New: Reflection Filtering** (Engine settings, off by default). Softens
  reflection shimmer on fine detail while you navigate. It adds roughly a second
  and a half to shader compile time and makes little difference to a finished
  still, so it is there for people who want a calmer preview rather than on for
  everyone.

## Rendering and export fixes

- **Restarting an image sequence part-way through the timeline now numbers the
  files correctly.** Starting a render at frame 690 saved them as `00000`
  onward, and a second run over a different range would overwrite the first
  because the names collided.
- **The sample progress bar shows the right total during a render.** A hi-res or
  video render counted its samples against the viewport's stop limit, so the bar
  sat at 100% almost immediately. It now shows the render's own budget and says
  which one you are looking at.
- **The Audio panel no longer halves your frame rate.** With the panel open and
  audio running, GMT dropped to about 30fps — and oddly ran faster when you
  clicked away. The spectrum display was repainting far more often than it
  needed to.

## Interface

- **Panel and menu animations work again.** The transitions were defined in a
  file the built app never loaded, so menus, popovers, toasts, the formula
  picker and the render dialog were all appearing instantly. Sliders and
  switches deliberately stay instant.

## Under the hood

Nothing here changes what you see, but it is why the release exists.

- Every subsystem audited, and every one of the 76 automated checks deliberately
  broken to confirm it actually fails when the thing it guards is broken. **44
  did not** — they had been passing for reasons unrelated to the code they were
  meant to protect. Those are fixed, and ten new checks were added.
- Roughly 1,900 lines of contracts and warnings written into the source itself,
  where the next person reads it, rather than into separate documents that go
  stale.
- Corrupt state now fails loudly at the point it happens instead of silently
  producing a wrong result later.
