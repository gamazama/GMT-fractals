# Deep zoom in GMT - the merged route

W = `H:/GMT/workspace-gmt/stable/.claude/worktrees/deep-zoom`. Page = `H:/GMT/workspace-gmt/mandelbulb_deepzoom/index.html` (branch `kernel-perf`).
Written 2026-10-07 by the judge of three plans, and revised the same day against the full input: all three plans (`W/debug/scratch/dz-wf2-plans.json`) and all twelve assumption checks (`W/debug/scratch/dz-wf2-checks.json`). Nothing was run; every code claim below comes from the planners' checks or the three studies beside this file (`HANDOFF-MAPPING.md`, `CORE-EXTRACTION.md`, `FORMULA-ELIGIBILITY.md`), which quote the symbols they read.

## Summary (10 lines)

1. Route: hand the scene across first (two tabs), then make the page a module, then show that module inside GMT's viewport, then save and animate it. Perturbation inside GMT's own marcher stays last and is not scheduled; its two deciding spikes (2 to 3 sessions, in the page) move up beside stage A.
2. Stage A (about 5 sessions): "Open in Deep Zoom" from a plain Mandelbulb scene in app-gmt. Same position, power, phase, gradient. Works on the level-1 iframe tool, no page change needed.
3. Stage K (about 1 session, in the page's repo): four small page handles - field of view, a smoothed-distance reset in `setView`, a pause, and three names the test suites read bare.
4. Stage X (3 to 4 sessions, in the page's repo): the page becomes `createDeepZoom(canvas, options)`. No new maths. `CORE-EXTRACTION.md` sizes it; it is much smaller than the "1 to 2 weeks" two planners assumed.
5. Stage D (1 day of measurement, then 5 to 7 sessions): deep mode in app-gmt's viewport. Gated on one GPU measurement: three WebGL contexts alive at once on real d3d11.
6. Stage E (about 1 week, 2 ADRs): the exact camera in scene files, a dive as one timeline track.
7. Stage F (native perturbation in GMT's marcher, Mandelbulb first): 2 to 3 sessions of spikes, then about 5 to 7 weeks to a Mandelbulb that resolves past 1e-6 with GMT's own marched shadows and AO, then about 4 to 6 weeks for reflections, fog, the path tracer and export. About 10 to 14 weeks in all; its planner said 7 to 11 before the checks added work. It stops near 3e-42; the page reaches 1e-77.
8. Limits through stage E: Mandelbulb only, whole-number power 2 to 16, Z Twist 0, Radiolaria off, no Julia, no hybrid, no pre/post rotation. GMT's path tracer, volumetrics and materials do not follow.
9. The picture changes at the hand-over: different lighting code, and the page's field of view is fixed at 34.1 degrees vertical against GMT's default 60 (1.88x tighter) until K1 lands.
10. What the full input changed: the order of stages stands. Native is cheaper and better founded than the first draft said (its shadow blocker is a cost question, not an accuracy one), but its first result is still weeks behind stage A's, for the same one formula. Read the spikes (F3, F4) before starting D4: GMT's lights in the page's shading pass is the work a native route would replace.

## The stages

### Stage 0 - level 1 lands (other team, in progress)
The `deep-zoom` engine app embedding the page in an iframe (`W/deep-zoom/**`, `W/deep-zoom.html`, `W/public/deep-zoom-core/index.html`). Everything below assumes its typecheck, build and GPU smoke are green.

### Stage A - hand a scene across (two tabs)
- **User gets:** File > "Open in Deep Zoom" in app-gmt. A new tab opens at the same place with the same power, phase and gradient 1, and keeps going from 1e-6 to about 1e-77. An ineligible scene gets a toast naming the reason (Z Twist, Radiolaria, Julia, hybrid, non-whole power, pre/post rotation, not a Mandelbulb). Later in the stage: "Open in GMT" from a saved deep location, landing at GMT's nearest safe distance (1e-5) on the same line of sight, and saying so.
- **Cost:** about 5 sessions (A1 to A5), plus one split-image check with the owner.
- **Must be true first:** stage 0 landed. Nothing in the page changes.
- **Known gaps on day one:** framing is 1.88x tighter than GMT's default (fixed by K1); camera roll is dropped and reported; a default scene's gradient 1 is a single white stop, so the deep tool keeps its own gradient unless the scene has a real one.

### Stage K - four small page handles
- **User gets:** matching framing after the hand-off; correct fly speed when a view is pushed into an already-running page; no GPU work from a hidden page.
- **Cost:** about 1 session in the page's repo, plus the page's own suites (`npm run verify`, per `CORE-EXTRACTION.md` section 10).
- **Must be true first:** the owner agrees that an agent may edit the page on `kernel-perf`, or does it himself (decision 2).
- Can run in parallel with stage A; A does not wait for it.

### Stage X - the page becomes a module
- **User gets:** nothing visible. The owner gets one master instead of a page plus a vendored copy, and GMT gets something it can mount on its own canvas.
- **Cost:** 3 to 4 sessions (`CORE-EXTRACTION.md` section 11): steps 1 to 5 mechanical in one session, UI split half a session, the factory wrap one session, host API one session.
- **Must be true first:** the owner's colour work on `kernel-perf` is at a stopping point; the suites can load the page over http (unverified - `CORE-EXTRACTION.md` step 0).
- Level 1's iframe keeps working throughout, because the `window.__` names stay behind a shim.
- **Three rules for the module, from check 8:** one driver only (inside GMT it is stepped from `registerTick`, never from its own `requestAnimationFrame` loop); its own wall clock (GMT clamps the tick's delta to 0.1 s); never a synchronous shader compile from a tick.

### Stage D - deep mode inside app-gmt's viewport
- **User gets:** on an eligible Mandelbulb scene, when the camera is near 1e-5, a HUD line offers "Continue in deep mode". The deep picture replaces GMT's in the same viewport; Formula power and phase and Gradient 1 keep driving it; leaving returns to GMT's marcher at a safe view as one undo step.
- **Cost:** 1 day of measurement (D0), then 5 to 7 sessions (D1 to D5).
- **Must be true first:** D0 says go; stage A's converter (A1) exists; stage K's pause and smoothed-distance reset exist. Built on the module (stage X) if it is ready, else on the iframe behind `?deep=1`.
- **If D0 says no-go:** deep mode unmounts GMT's renderer while active (a full worker restart on return, seconds, accumulation lost), or stays a separate tab. Stages A, K, X are unaffected.
- **Known cost:** the deep shader's first compile is about 15 s. On Chrome it runs off the main thread; on Firefox (no `KHR_parallel_shader_compile`) the page freezes for that long once (check 8, decision 7).

### Stage E - saves and the timeline
- **User gets:** a GMT scene saved in deep mode reopens at the exact view; a recorded dive is one track on GMT's timeline that can be scrubbed.
- **Cost:** about 1 week, two ADRs (string-encoded exact camera field; a float track that indexes app-owned pose data). No engine type change and no new track type is needed (check 7).
- **Must be true first:** stage D. Mixed GMT-and-deep video export stays research (E3) and is not sized.

### Stage F - perturbation in GMT's own marcher (not scheduled; its two spikes are)
- **The idea (native plan).** A compile-gated mode of GMT's marcher that measures every length in units of S = 2^`uRefE`, with S close to the surface distance. f32 then keeps its 7 digits around the camera at any depth. GMT's secondary rays are already camera-relative - `DE` / `DE_Dist` in `W/engine-gmt/shaders/chunks/de.ts` add `uCameraPosition` afterwards - so swapping those two wrappers for a perturbed `mapPert` carries primary rays, shadows, AO and reflections together. With the gate off the shader must be byte-identical to today's.
- **User gets, in four steps:**
  1. *(F5 to F14)* In app-gmt, on a plain Mandelbulb, a "Deep zoom" mode toggle that recompiles once. The surface keeps resolving past 1e-6 with GMT's lights, soft shadows and AO in Direct mode. No change of look, no second renderer. Reach: about 1e-13 on the camera as written, about 1e-25 once the camera fix (F8) is in.
  2. *(F15, F16)* Reflections, fog, depth of field, fly speed, the path tracer, the tiled progressive renderer and export behave at depth as they do at 1e-3. The second-renderer route does not give these at any stage.
  3. *(F17)* On to the scaled frame's ceiling, about 3e-42, with the exact view in the scene file and a dive on the timeline - the same string field and track as stage E.
  4. *(F18)* A second and third formula, and a badge in the formula picker for which formulas can go deep.
- **Honest size:** spikes 2 to 3 sessions. Step 1: the planner's 4 to 6 weeks (12 to 16 sessions) plus about 4 to 5 sessions the checks added (camera fix, precision floor, far limits, distance adapter, the Physics probe) - about 5 to 7 weeks. Step 2: the planner's 3 to 5 weeks plus about 10 absolute-position sites the checks found - about 4 to 6 weeks. Steps 1 and 2 together about 10 to 14 weeks, which is the first investigation's "2 to 4 months" (`W/debug/scratch/dz-wf1-result-1.txt`) reached from the other side. The added sessions are my estimate, not the planner's. Step 3: 2 to 3 weeks, less whatever stage E already built. Step 4: 1 to 3 weeks per smooth formula; fold formulas have no size.
- **Must be true first:** both spikes pass in the standalone page - F3 (the bulb's kernel still certifies when the march runs in plain f32 in units of 2^`uRefE`) and F4 (a marched shadow ray from a deep hit point agrees with the referee). Owner decision 1. Preferably stage X's maths modules (X1), so GMT imports the page's BigInt code instead of keeping a second copy.
- **If F4 fails:** the route collapses to deep primary rays with shading from the depth buffer, which is stages A to E with a second shading path inside GMT. Stage F stops there.
- **What the checks changed** (evidence in items 9 to 12 below):
  - The shadow question moved from "open problem" to "mechanism sound, unmeasured". The page skipped marched shadows for cost, not accuracy. The cost stays: about one extra full march per shadowed light, so deep marched shadows are for stills and accumulation, not live flight.
  - The one-swap claim holds for every marched secondary query. The real step-2 work is elsewhere: about 10 sites that rebuild an absolute position in f32, and `NUMERIC_DE` formulas, which are excluded until reworked.
  - A plain float `t` is enough, so `trace.ts` and `shadows.ts` are not rewritten. But the trace loop is not untouched: its precision floor, `MAX_DIST` / `MISS_DIST`, and the kernel's distance output all need deep-mode versions. The ceiling is about 3e-42, not 1e-38.
  - The six-number camera does not give 1e-25. As written it gives about 1e-16 of position, enough for a surface distance of about 1e-13. A small change to `VirtualSpace` (days) lifts that to about 1e-32.
- **Limits that stay:** of 48 formula files, 1 is a port (Mandelbulb, whole-number power, twist and Radiolaria off, no hybrid - the same envelope as stage A), 2 to 5 are plausible new derivations, and the fold and IFS majority has no known route, so the toggle is greyed out on most of the catalogue. The work lands in shader chunks, which are strings typecheck cannot see, and cold path-traced compiles already run 10 to 20 s.
- **Planned now:** F0 (a gallery count of which formulas people use), F3 and F4 (the spikes), and F1 (the per-formula contract proved on Mandelbar3D, the easiest maths - `FORMULA-ELIGIBILITY.md` section 3). All four inform decision 1.

#### The case for native first
The native plan argues for going straight to the marcher. It is the only route where the look does not change at 1e-6; where reflections, fog, the path tracer, materials and export follow the user down; and where there is no second renderer, no third WebGL context and no suspend gate to build. Its two spikes cost 2 to 3 sessions and need nothing from GMT. The checks strengthened it: the shadow blocker is a cost question, not an accuracy one (item 9), and the marcher keeps its float loop (item 11).

Judgement: not first, but earlier than the first draft had it. Against it: the first thing a user can touch is 5 to 7 weeks away instead of 5 sessions; it is one formula in the same restricted form stage A serves; it stops near 3e-42 where the page reaches 1e-77, and near 1e-13 until `VirtualSpace` is changed; and a mistake in shader strings is the hardest kind to see. Little in stages A, K, X or E is wasted if native follows: A1's eligibility test, X1's maths modules, X5's orbit worker and E1's string camera field are reused by F14, F7, F10 and F17. What native would replace is D4, and it would leave stage D serving only the depths below native's ceiling. So: run F3 and F4 beside stage A and read the result before starting D4. If both pass and the owner wants GMT's look at depth more than he wants 1e-77 in the viewport, step 1 of stage F takes the place of D4 and D5 (decision 1).

## What the checks changed

Hand-off plan:
1. **"Same place in both renderers" - partly.** Same map, axes and handedness hold (`formula_Mandelbulb` in `W/engine-gmt/formulas/Mandelbulb.ts` against the page's `deMandelbulb`; `basis()` at yaw -pi/2, pitch 0 is an identity three.js camera). Failed: field of view (page fixed at 34.1 degrees; the planner's "63" was a misreading of `uv.y` spanning +-0.5), roll (page has yaw and pitch only), and rotations (`applyPreRotation` / `applyPostRotation` in `W/engine-gmt/shaders/chunks/math.ts` run inside the loop). Consequence: A1 reads `optics.camFov`, drops and reports roll, and refuses pre/post rotation; K1 adds a FOV setter. Which store fields feed the rotation matrices is unverified and is A1's first step.
2. **"Iframe layer inside the viewport" - partly.** Mounting, sizing and keyboard separation hold. Failed: it is three WebGL contexts, not two (worker canvas, R3F overlay, page); `setIsPaused` is soft (`FractalEngine` keeps rendering about 1 s after any wake, bucket renders ignore it); the page has no pause handle. Consequence: D0 is a measurement, not a build; D1 adds a real suspend gate; K3 adds a page pause.
3. **"`setView` applies a view fully" - partly.** The 2^256 fixed-point format holds exactly (`makeHP`, `fromNum`). Failed: `setView` does not touch `smoothedDist`, which only rises at 1.2/s, so a jump to a shallower view in a running page flies slowly and may stay in deep mode for seconds; auto iterations only ever rise. Consequence: the converter always sends `iters`, power and phase; K2 resets the smoothed distance. A fresh tab starts at 1.0, so stage A does not need K2.
4. **"A preset fragment can be applied at runtime" - partly.** `applyPartialPreset` (`W/engine-gmt/utils/applyPartialPreset.ts`) does not carry the camera and resets every other param of a listed feature to default. Consequence: the return trip (A5, D3) calls feature setters with only the changed keys inside one param transaction, and writes the camera by mirroring `loadPreset`'s `CAMERA_TELEPORT` + `OFFSET_SET` emits. `gradientAt` returns null for a flat gradient, so A3 uses its own reader.

Module plan:

Consequence: K4 exports the bare names first; X3 turns the 6 sites into options; the handles are namespaced on the instance with a shim for the standalone page.

Traps: `getDiff` and `quantize` drop any key that starts with `is`; `presetFieldRegistry` freezes at store construction (`PresetFieldFrozenError`); the binder-first lookup is marked `@assumption` in the source, not `@invariant`. Native plan:
9. **"The bulb's kernel stays accurate for shadow, AO and bounce points on the camera's one reference orbit" - mechanism sound, unmeasured.** Supports it (all in the page): `pertOrbit(sampler2D orbit, int orbitLen, Hdr3 dc, int maxIter)` takes any 3-vector delta; the normal taps (`dp = t*rd + eH*kv`) and the population probe (`__popStudy`, refereed against `pertOrbitJS`) already feed it off-ray deltas; the rebase rule (`if (z2 < dot(dz, dz) || m >= orbitLen - 1)`) does not depend on delta size; and the primary march already uses the kernel out to `T_SWITCH = 1e-3`, about 1e22 surface distances at a 1e-25 view. The page's stated reason for depth-buffer shadows is cost ("a real shadow ray here would be about a second march per shadowed light"), with no accuracy objection in the code or its HANDOFF. Not proven: no test evaluates a ray that starts at a hit point; the near march has a 256-step budget (`exhausted`) that a grazing shadow ray would eat; the delta resolves about 6e-8 of its own length, so a fixed-epsilon shadow or AO test loses it beyond roughly 1e4 surface distances (the checker's arithmetic, untested); a non-escaped point returns a garbage distance and must count as a hit, a rule `GetSoftShadow` does not know. Consequence: F4 is a hard gate with its pass criteria written down before it runs; deep marched shadows are a stills feature; secondary rays need the same near-to-far hand-off at 1e-3; shadow and AO epsilons must grow with distance from the camera.
10. **"Every secondary-ray distance query goes through `DE` / `DE_Dist`, so one swap converts them all" - holds for the queries, not for what surrounds them.** Holds: every marched secondary query found uses the wrappers in `W/engine-gmt/shaders/chunks/de.ts` - `lighting/shadows.ts` (2 sites), `pathtracer.ts` (1), `GetNormal` / `GetFastNormal` in `material_eval.ts`, `W/engine-gmt/features/reflections/shader.ts` (3), `features/ao/shader.ts` (1), `features/lighting/index.ts` (3). The first investigation's list of direct `map()` callers in `pbr.ts`, `volumetric_scatter.ts` and `shading.ts` was wrong: they have none. The primary march has 3 direct sites in `trace.ts`, not 13, plus the `variant === 'Physics'` `traceScene` in `W/engine-gmt/engine/ShaderBuilder.ts`. Failed in three places: `numericNormal(p_ray + uCameraPosition, eps)` under `NUMERIC_DE` in `material_eval.ts` bypasses the wrapper; `numFootprint` in `de.ts` assumes an absolute point; and about 10 sites rebuild `p + uCameraPosition + uSceneOffsetLow + uSceneOffsetHigh` in f32 (`p_fractal`, `distFromFractalOrigin`, `worldOriginOffset`, `_pfrac`), several of them feeding precision thresholds. Unverified: what each consumer does with the value, and whether a feature injects more `map()` calls through `addPostDEFunction`. Consequence: no per-call-site conversion in step 2. F12 routes the 4 primary sites through the wrappers; F15 takes the 10 position sites as its own line item; `NUMERIC_DE` formulas are excluded from native deep mode (the Mandelbulb is analytic, so it is unaffected).
11. **"A plain f32 march in units of 2^uRefE is enough" - holds for the loop variable, fails for "the loops need no change".** Holds (the check read `marchNear` in full): the page's `vec2 t` has a one-f32 mantissa (`hdrToFloat`), so it carries no more digits than a float, and the hit test, bisection stop and step are all ratios that give the same answer when `t` and the distance are both divided by 2^`uRefE`. The page needs the pair only because it marches in absolute units out to 1e-3, which is 74 decades at a 1e-77 view. Failed: in `getTraceGLSL` (`W/engine-gmt/shaders/chunks/trace.ts`) `floatPrecision = max(1.0e-20, distFromFractalOrigin * PRECISION_RATIO_HIGH)` becomes about 5e18 in scaled units and makes every ray hit on step 0; `MAX_DIST 10000.0` / `MISS_DIST 1000.0` (`W/engine-gmt/shaders/chunks/math.ts`) stop a ray after 10,000 surface distances and call any hit past 1,000 sky; the kernel's distance comes out as a pair that overflows f32; and `trace.ts` emits its own `map(p + uCameraPosition)` calls (`innerDistCall`, the miss block). The ceiling is about 3e-42 (scaled `t` at the 1e-3 hand-off overflowing f32), tighter than the planner's 1e-38. Consequence: no two-component rewrite of `trace.ts` or `shadows.ts`, so the "step 1 doubles to 2 to 3 months" branch does not apply. F9 gains a distance adapter; F12 gains the floor and the far limits; F3 also tests a grazing view. No GPU run was done, so F3 is still the proof.
12. **"The six-number camera gives about 1e-25 end to end" - fails as stated.** Holds: the six numbers cross to the worker intact (`SerializedOffset` in `W/engine-gmt/engine/worker/WorkerProtocol.ts`; `OFFSET_SET` carries the object, `OFFSET_SHIFT` a double delta), and `split` in `W/engine/plugins/navigation/core/VirtualSpace.ts` is an exact split. Failed: `normalize` only carries whole units (`THRESHOLD = 0.5`) and `move` / `absorbCamera` add all travel into the low word, so the low word is an ordinary double of size up to 0.5 with a resolution near 5.5e-17; `setFromUnified` takes one double per axis; `updateShaderUniforms` sums high, low and camera in a single double before splitting again. The 1e-24 figure is true only straight after a split and within about 1e-7 of an f32 grid point. `updateSmoothing` also has absolute thresholds (`1.0e-18` and `1.0e-21` squared distance) that stop discriminating below about 1e-10. Consequence: on the camera as written, step 1 reaches a surface distance of about 1e-13 (position must be good to about S/1000) - still 7 orders past today's wall. F8 (new) makes the pair a real double-double, about 1e-32, in days; if that is refused, F17 becomes a prerequisite of step 1. Either way the orbit centre and the camera-minus-reference delta are computed on the CPU from the high and low words separately (F10), never from `uSceneOffsetHigh` + `uSceneOffsetLow`.

## Ruled out, and why

- **Sharing GMT's canvas.** `WorkerProxy.initWorkerMode` calls `transferControlToOffscreen`, which is one-shot. A second canvas is the only option.
- **Using `proxy.isPaused` to idle GMT.** It wakes on interaction and ticks are still posted every frame.
- **`applyPartialPreset` for the return trip.** Resets the user's other params; does not move the camera.
- **A whole-scene load on return, as the normal path.** `loadPreset` clears undo history and the dirty baseline. Kept only as a last resort.
- **Shipping an iframe inside app-gmt as the product.** Acceptable as the D0 measurement rig and as a flagged fallback only; with extraction at 3 to 4 sessions, the module is the product path.
- **Waiting for native perturbation before giving the user anything.** 5 to 7 weeks behind two spikes, for one formula, against 5 sessions for stage A.
- **Porting the page's GL layer into a worker with OffscreenCanvas (2 to 3 weeks).** No guard forces it, and it does not fix the real stall, which is the shared GPU process (check 8).
- **An engine type extension or a new track type for the exact camera.** Not needed: strings ride `PresetField` and any id can be a float track (check 7).
- **A mantissa-and-exponent `t` inside GMT's `trace.ts` and `shadows.ts`.** Not needed down to about 3e-42 (check 11). Going below that is research and is not sized.
- **Promising native deep zoom to 1e-25 on today's camera.** `VirtualSpace` delivers a surface distance of about 1e-13 as written (check 12).
- **Native deep mode on `NUMERIC_DE` formulas.** `numericNormal` and `numFootprint` bypass the wrappers the whole design rests on (check 10).
- **Copying `explorerHandoff.ts` for the deep-zoom trip.** Against "one source of truth"; A2 writes one generic helper. Whether the shipped GX trip moves onto it now or later is decision 6.
- **MengerSponge and other pure IFS formulas as deep-zoom targets.** Exactly self-similar; a wrap-around zoom shows the same thing (`FORMULA-ELIGIBILITY.md`).
- **Non-integer power and Z Twist in deep mode.** The page's kernel covers integer powers 2 to 16 with phase only.
